## 2026-07-29 — Initial falsification

Tested the implicit hypothesis: “higher checklist score means higher organizational capability.” It fails conceptually because many high scores encode a particular practice, tool, or workflow while omitting customer outcome and context. The instrument also supplies no calibration or criterion study supporting the −1-to-3 ordering.

## 2026-07-29 — Evidence synthesis

- Anchored assessment standards in AERA/APA/NCME and COSMIN.
- Used DORA as the strongest available software-delivery research program while retaining its observational and contextual limits.
- Used SPACE to reject one-dimensional productivity/activity measurement.
- Reviewed public descriptions of Scrum.org EBM, Agile Fluency, Google team-effectiveness research, and common organizational survey bias/IRT literature.
- Distinguished absence of public validation from evidence of no value.

## 2026-07-29 — Redesign iteration 1

Drafted a balanced outcome/capability model. Rejected a total weighted score because weights would encode unstated utility and allow compensation (for example, customer value masking unsafe operations).

## 2026-07-29 — Redesign iteration 2

Reduced the core to 36 behaviorally anchored perceptions and 12 evidence requests. Added a uniform 90-day referent, “not observable,” role distributions, and adaptive root-cause follow-ups. Kept positive wording for clarity but mitigated acquiescence through evidence triangulation; reverse-worded items were not added because they often introduce method factors and confusion.

## 2026-07-29 — Redesign iteration 3

Added explicit rival hypotheses, evidence confidence, perception–reality gaps, privacy constraints, and prohibited uses. Reframed Clarity as separable observable constructs. Used EDF only as a provisional diagnostic loop because no authoritative definition was available.

## Remaining uncertainty and stopping rule

The literature review is targeted rather than a preregistered systematic review, and several commercial assessment methods are proprietary. The new items have not undergone cognitive interviewing or calibration. Further desk editing now has lower expected value than empirical testing, so iteration stops at a research prototype and transfers effort to the validation plan.
